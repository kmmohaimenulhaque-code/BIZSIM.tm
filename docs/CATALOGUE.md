# BIZSIM season catalogue

**200 seasons · 800 episodes · 1600 decisions** in six pillars. Every episode is made of two linked decisions and ends with its own outcome and insight. All 200 seasons are built in and play offline, with no AI key needed. ★ = signature season (the original ten, with extra hand-written depth); ✚ = added by the BIZSIM team (131–150); ◆ = faith-era and modern strategy expansion (151–200). Bonus seasons 121–130 come from your curriculum. Each season cites its Wikipedia articles, and the cover image is the lead image of the season’s own Wikipedia article (free licences only).

Live company research makes a season too: episodes I to III replay documented history, two or three decisions each; episode IV holds the decisions the company faces now (marked **Live**). The chat builder turns a plain-language request into up to three seasons, grounded in Wikipedia.
